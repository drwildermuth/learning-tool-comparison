Tool Comparison Table

Using this table is optional. It is here to make your report easier. Fill it in, download it as a Word document, and copy it into your report.

How to use it:
  1. In the first two columns, choose the two new tools you tried.
  2. For every option, type the price of an individual license and the web address where you found it.
  3. Rate each option from 1 to 5 on each criterion (1 is poor, 5 is excellent), and add a few words of evidence.
  4. If you want, click Add your own criterion to add a row, such as accessibility or support.
  5. Click Download as Word.
Before you stop for the day: your work saves in your browser on this device, so use the same browser and device when you come back. Download your table when you are finished. The Word table is sized for a portrait page, so it pastes straight into your report.
